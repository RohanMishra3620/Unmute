About {{ app_name }}

It's okay to feel. It's okay to talk.

What is Unmute?

Unmute is a quiet place to talk about how you are feeling. It is made for people who are going through a hard time, such as victims of atrocities who are waiting for an investigation, a trial or compensation, and who may not have someone to talk to every day.

You chat for a few minutes, in simple words. At the end, you get a short reflection of what you shared. The idea is to notice changes in your well-being early, so that the right human support can reach you in time.

How your {{ minutes }}-minute session works

  1. Talk freely. Type in your own words, or tap a quick reply. There are no right or wrong answers.
  2. We listen for your feelings. The chat looks at the words you use to understand stress, fear, worry or low mood, and checks for your safety.
  3. See your reflection. You get a simple report with the feelings noticed and a few small steps you can try.

Your privacy

We do not ask for your name, phone number or address. You can delete your session and report at any time from the report page.

What Unmute is not

Unmute is an AI support tool. It is not a doctor, therapist or emergency service, and it does not give a diagnosis. This is an early prototype.
